## Sources and interpretation

- Delegation diagrams use a separate recorded engineering return. Research history is another recorded case; the edit does not claim they are one execution.
- Kanban and controls use a published workspace scenario. Trajectory analysis uses synthetic packaged-UI data. Its six process judgments are uncalibrated and do not form a total score. Citation paths are illustrated.
- The graph record used two CPU threads and generated full-size joint-neighborhood graphs at30% partial duplication: accuracy41.2%→85.0%. The independent-edge control kept its accuracy while time rose32%. This supports testing nonlinear candidates; native score gain remains unknown. It is not a causal LoopX-versus-baseline result.
- The watermarked Seedance2.5 concept insert reuses earlier material; new diagrams are drawn in code. No new remote generation or benchmark execution.
- Enterprise and research organizations describe the intended audience, not verified deployment, security, SLA or multitenancy. Historical1.0.3 LHTB results retain their original version.
